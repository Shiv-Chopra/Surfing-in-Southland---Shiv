Surfing in Southland

Southland is one of New Zealand's hidden surfing destinations. With rugged coastlines, consistent swells and uncrowded beaches, it offers fantastic waves for surfers of all experience levels. From beginner-friendly beaches to powerful reef breaks, Southland has something for everyone who enjoys the ocean.

Colac Bay

Southland's most popular surf break with consistent waves and beautiful scenery.

Oreti Beach

A long sandy beach offering beginner-friendly waves and plenty of space.

Porpoise Bay

A scenic beach famous for dolphins and enjoyable surfing conditions.

Cosy Nook

A quieter surf spot with stunning coastal views and quality waves.

Which Beach Should You Visit?

If you're new to surfing, Oreti Beach is a great place to start because it has long, open waves and plenty of space. More experienced surfers often head to Colac Bay, which is famous for its reliable left-hand point break. If you're looking for beautiful scenery and a quieter surfing experience, Porpoise Bay and Cosy Nook are excellent choices.
